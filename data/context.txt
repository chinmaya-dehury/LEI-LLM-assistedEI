This sample dataset represents environmental conditions inside a smart building’s office room.
The IoT edge device collects temperature and humidity readings every 5 minutes.
The purpose is to monitor human comfort and detect any abnormal variations that may indicate cooling system malfunction, humidity rise, or poor ventilation.
Typical insights could include (but not limited to):
- Estimating comfort index using temperature and humidity
- Detecting sudden changes or anomalies
- Calculating hourly averages
- Predicting next-hour conditions
- Triggering alerts if comfort thresholds are exceeded

The LLM should generate small, efficient Python programs suitable for execution on the edge device.
Each program should focus on one insight at a time and be optimized for limited computational resources.
