This sample dataset represents soil conditions at various depths for agricultural or environmental monitoring.
The IoT edge device collects soil temperature and moisture readings at multiple depth levels every hour.
The purpose is to monitor soil health, irrigation needs, and root zone conditions for optimal crop management or ecosystem analysis.

Typical insights could include (but not limited to):
- Soil moisture gradient analysis – water distribution across depth layers (0-1cm to 27-81cm)
- Temperature stratification – thermal profile from surface (0cm) to deeper layers (54cm)
- Irrigation efficiency – detecting moisture penetration depth after watering events
- Drought stress detection – identifying low moisture zones critical for plant roots
- Temporal moisture trends – daily or seasonal soil water availability patterns
- Optimal watering time recommendation – based on surface vs subsurface moisture balance
- Root zone health assessment – moisture and temperature suitability for plant growth
- Evaporation rate estimation – surface moisture loss patterns

The LLM should generate small, efficient Python programs suitable for execution on the edge device.
Each program should focus on one insight at a time and be optimized for limited computational resources.
