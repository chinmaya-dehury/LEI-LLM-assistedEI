This dataset represents soil conditions at various depths for agricultural and environmental monitoring, including soil temperature and moisture measurements critical for crop management.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for soil health and irrigation management, measuring soil temperature and volumetric moisture content at multiple depth levels hourly.

The objective is to support irrigation optimization, drought detection, root zone assessment, and crop yield prediction for precision agriculture and farm management systems.

The deployment environment includes edge devices (Raspberry Pi 4, quad-core ARM, 1-4GB RAM) in agricultural fields with periodic cloud synchronization and offline operation capability.

Useful analytical insights may include:
- Soil moisture gradient analysis across depth layers (0-1cm, 1-3cm, 9-27cm)
- Soil temperature stratification and thermal profile dynamics
- Irrigation efficiency and water penetration depth assessment
- Drought stress detection and early warning thresholds
- Temporal moisture trends for predictive irrigation scheduling
- Root zone health assessment based on temperature-moisture balance
- Seasonal variation analysis for multi-year agricultural planning
- Anomaly detection for sensor malfunction or irrigation system issues

The LLM should generate small, efficient, and modular Python programs suitable for execution on resource-constrained edge devices.

Each generated task should focus on a single analytical insight while avoiding duplicate or computationally expensive operations.
