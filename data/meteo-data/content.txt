This dataset represents high-altitude alpine meteorological telemetry monitoring and sensing parameters.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for real-time weather observation, microclimate analysis, and environmental parameter tracking.

The objective is to support atmospheric temperature profiling, solar radiation estimations, relative humidity trends, soil moisture hydrology monitoring, and wind vector analytics.

The deployment environment includes a remote high-altitude alpine weather station recording continuously over time.

Useful analytical insights may include:
- Ambient and surface temperature correlation studies to monitor diurnal heating and cooling.
- Solar radiation modeling to calculate daily exposure cycles and estimate cloud cover impact.
- Relative humidity and soil moisture tracking for microclimatic hydrology and watermark tension analysis.
- Wind vector calculation combining wind speed and wind direction for wind pattern profiling and draft detection.
- Rain meter profiling to track precipitation events and evaluate correlation with atmospheric variables.

The LLM should generate small, efficient, and modular Python programs suitable for execution on resource-constrained edge devices.

Each generated task should focus on a single analytical insight while avoiding duplicate or computationally expensive operations.
