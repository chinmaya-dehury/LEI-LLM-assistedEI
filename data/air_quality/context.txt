This dataset represents air quality measurements collected from IoT sensor networks deployed in urban and rural locations monitoring real-time pollution levels.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for air pollution monitoring, measuring six key pollutants (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2, CO) at 10-15 minute intervals.

The objective is to support air quality assessment, public health warnings, and pollution trend analysis for environmental monitoring systems.

The deployment environment includes low-power edge devices (Raspberry Pi 4 / ARM Cortex-A53, 512MB-2GB RAM) with periodic cloud synchronization and integration with air quality dashboards.

Useful analytical insights may include:
- Air Quality Index (AQI) level trends and category classification (Good, Moderate, Unhealthy, etc.)
- Main pollutant identification for current air quality conditions
- Peak pollution hour detection and temporal pattern analysis
- Source inference based on pollutant signature patterns
- Seasonal variation analysis and long-term pollution trends
- Anomaly detection for sensor drift or unusual pollution spikes
- Health risk mapping and vulnerable population alerts

The LLM should generate small, efficient, and modular Python programs suitable for execution on resource-constrained edge devices.

Each generated task should focus on a single analytical insight while avoiding duplicate or computationally expensive operations.
