This dataset represents hourly averaged responses from an array of 5 metal oxide chemical sensors embedded in an Air Quality Chemical Multisensor Device.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for air quality monitoring and gas concentration estimation at road level.

The objective is to support on-field air pollution assessment, identify environmental patterns, and support ground truth validation.

The deployment environment includes a significantly polluted field area at road level within an Italian city, recorded from March 2004 to February 2005.

Useful analytical insights may include:
- Estimation of CO, Non Metanic Hydrocarbons (NMHC), Benzene (C6H6), NOx, and NO2 concentrations compared against certified co-located reference analyzer ground truth.
- Identification of sensor cross-sensitivities under varying temperature and relative/absolute humidity conditions.
- Detection of both concept and sensor drifts over the year-long deployment timeline.

The LLM should generate small, efficient, and modular Python programs suitable for execution on resource-constrained edge devices.

Each generated task should focus on a single analytical insight while avoiding duplicate or computationally expensive operations.
