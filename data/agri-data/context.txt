This dataset represents a smart farming parameter monitoring dataset across critical soil conditions, ambient atmospheric parameters, and crop-related metrics.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for precision agriculture, crop monitoring, and environmental stress analysis.

The objective is to support precision crop prediction, environmental stress analysis, crop health classification, and agricultural resource management optimization.

The deployment environment includes smart farms with varying soil types (Sandy, Loamy, Clay) and different growth stages (Seedling, Vegetative, Flowering).

Useful analytical insights may include:
- Precision crop prediction and classification (e.g., matching NPK, temperature, and soil moisture to labels).
- Composite environmental stress analysis using derived features like Temperature-Humidity Index (THI) and Photosynthesis Potential (PP).
- Resource management optimization by analyzing Fertilizer Usage, Irrigation Frequency, and Water Usage Efficiency (L/kg).
- Composite soil health and fertility assessment using Soil Fertility Index (SFI) and Nutrient Balance Ratio (NBR).

The LLM should generate small, efficient, and modular Python programs suitable for execution on resource-constrained edge devices.

Each generated task should focus on a single analytical insight while avoiding duplicate or computationally expensive operations.
