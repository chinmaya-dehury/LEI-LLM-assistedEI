This dataset represents wind conditions at multiple altitude levels for weather monitoring and wind energy assessment, including wind speed, direction, and gust measurements.

The IoT edge device continuously collects sensor readings for wind resource monitoring, measuring wind speed and direction at different heights (10m, 80m) with gust data at hourly intervals.

The objective is to support wind energy optimization, weather forecasting, and wind resource assessment for renewable energy and meteorological applications.

The deployment environment includes edge devices (Raspberry Pi 4, quad-core ARM, 1-4GB RAM) at weather stations or wind farm facilities with periodic cloud synchronization.

- Optimal height recommendation for wind turbine placement
- Diurnal wind cycle patterns comparing day and night conditions
- Wind persistence analysis for sustained energy generation periods

- Seasonal wind resource variation and monthly trend analysis
